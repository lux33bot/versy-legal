What we collect

Your account details, the entries you post, the votes you cast, and how you use the app (which screens, how long you watch). Your birth year is stored privately and never shown.

To be completed by counsel: Full data inventory incl. device identifiers, push tokens, crash logs.

How we use it

To run contests fairly, to rank matchups, to personalise the For You feed, and to keep the app safe. The Following feed is never personalised.

To be completed by counsel: Legal bases (GDPR), CCPA categories and purposes.

Who we share it with

Service providers that host video (Mux), run our database (Supabase) and deliver notifications (Expo). We do not sell personal data.

To be completed by counsel: Processor list, international transfers, law-enforcement requests.

Your choices

Delete your account from Settings at any time: your profile, entries and media are removed; votes you cast stay, without your name. Turn off personalised recommendations in Settings.

To be completed by counsel: Access/portability/correction rights, opt-out mechanics, children’s data (COPPA), retention periods.

To be completed by counsel: Controller identity, privacy contact, EU/UK representative if applicable.
