What we collect

Your account details, the entries you post, the votes you cast, and how you use the app (which screens, how long you watch). Your birth year is stored privately and never shown.

In detail: your email address and sign-in details (or your Apple ID sign-in, if you use it); your handle, display name, bio and photo; your birth year, and the age range your phone’s app store shares when it does (Apple or Google: under 13, 13–15, 16–17 or 18 and over — used only to apply the age rules, and the stricter of the two counts); the photos and videos you post with their titles and captions; your votes, comments, follows, blocks and reports; the messages you send (text, photos and posts you share into a chat), your reactions to messages and when you last read each chat; the categories you follow; the notification token of each device you sign in on and its platform (iOS or Android); and usage events such as what was shown to you, how long you watched, and upload or error events with the time they happened. If you joined from a link someone shared, which account shared it.

When the app crashes or hits an error, a crash report: the error and where in the app it happened, your phone’s model and system version, the app version, and the app’s last steps before it (such as which of our servers it was talking to) — without your name, email, account or the details of what you were doing.

Versy uses your camera, microphone and photo library only when you record or pick something to post. We do not collect your location or contacts and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

How we use it

To run contests fairly, to rank matchups, to personalise For You (the feed, and which contest comes next when voting on For You), and to keep the app safe. The Following feed, matchups inside a contest, and accounts under 18 are never personalised.

Your vote history is also used to detect vote rings and bots, which keeps results fair for everyone. Votes that look suspicious are shown to a moderator, who can remove the ones found to be fake.

Links you share carry a code for your account (accounts under 18 get none). When someone 18 or over with a public profile joins Versy from your link, or enters the contest you challenged them to with Challenge a friend, you see their @handle in Activity; the pushes for these can be turned off in Settings → Notifications. If you enter a contest from someone’s challenge link, they are told the same way (only if you are 18 or over with a public profile), and Download my data lists it.

Sometimes we try two versions of a screen to learn which works better. Adults are split at random, and we compare only totals, such as how many people came back the next day. Accounts under 18, and accounts we can’t yet be sure are 18 or over, are never part of a test: they see the standard version until the test ends, then whichever version is kept. Download my data lists the tests you were part of.

Who we share it with

Anyone who opens a link to an entry or a profile sees a preview on our website, without signing in: for an entry, its picture (for a video, a still from it), title and contest and the creator’s @handle, name and photo; for a profile, the @handle, name, photo, number of crowns and latest contest entries. Previews show only public accounts of people we know are 18 or over, and only entries our moderators have passed that were posted once we knew that — never your bio, followers, votes, messages, My VS posts or This or That. Private accounts, accounts under 18 and accounts whose age we can’t confirm get no preview.

Service providers that host video (Mux), run our database (Supabase), deliver notifications (Expo), receive crash reports so we can fix errors (Sentry) and check uploads for known child sexual abuse material (Project Arachnid Shield, run by the Canadian Centre for Child Protection). We do not sell personal data.

Supabase stores the database, sign-in, photos and messages (message photos are kept private: only the two people in the chat — and our moderators, for a reported message — can open them); Mux stores and streams video, and makes captions from the speech in each video (shown to people who turn on captions); Expo delivers notifications and app updates; Apple handles Sign in with Apple and iPhone notifications. They process data only to provide these services to us. Project Arachnid Shield receives each photo, and stills from each video and a 24-hour link to the whole video, to compare them with known child sexual abuse material. When you sign up, log in with email or reset your password, Cloudflare Turnstile checks in the background that a person, not a bot, is signing in; Cloudflare’s Turnstile Privacy Addendum (cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy) says what it collects for that. Your data may be processed in the United States.

We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it or to protect someone’s safety.

Your choices

Delete your account from Settings at any time: your profile, entries and media are removed; votes you cast stay, without your name. For a feed that is not personalised, use Following on Home: it shows the people you follow in time order.

Download a copy of your data from Settings → Account security → Download my data (it includes the messages you sent). Correct your details in Edit profile.

Messages: unsending a message erases its text or photo for both people — if someone has already reported it, our moderators keep it, out of the chat, until they have reviewed the report. Deleting a chat removes it from your inbox only. When either person deletes their account, the chat between them is deleted for both, photos included. “Typing…” and “Seen” are passed between the two phones and never stored.

How long we keep things: read notifications 90 days (any notification 180 days); feed history 30 days; everything tied to your account until you delete it. When you delete your account, usage events are kept only without anything linking them to you, so daily totals stay correct.

Versy is not for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, tell us and we will delete it.
