What we collect

Your account details, the entries you post, the votes you cast, and how you use the app (which screens, how long you watch). Your birth year is stored privately and never shown.

In detail: your email address and sign-in details (or your Apple ID sign-in, if you use it); your handle, display name, bio and photo; your birth year; the photos and videos you post with their titles and captions; your votes, comments, follows, blocks and reports; the categories you follow; the notification token of each device you sign in on and its platform (iOS or Android); and usage events such as what was shown to you, how long you watched, and upload or error events with the time they happened.

Versy uses your camera, microphone and photo library only when you record or pick something to post. We do not collect your location or contacts and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

How we use it

To run contests fairly, to rank matchups, to personalise the For You feed, and to keep the app safe. The Following feed is never personalised.

Your vote history is also used to detect vote rings and bots, which keeps results fair for everyone.

To be completed by counsel: Legal bases where GDPR applies; CCPA categories.

Who we share it with

Service providers that host video (Mux), run our database (Supabase) and deliver notifications (Expo). We do not sell personal data.

Supabase stores the database, sign-in and photos; Mux stores and streams video; Expo delivers notifications and app updates; Apple handles Sign in with Apple and iPhone notifications. They process data only to provide these services to us. Your data may be processed in the United States.

We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it or to protect someone’s safety.

Your choices

Delete your account from Settings at any time: your profile, entries and media are removed; votes you cast stay, without your name. For a feed that is not personalised, use Following on Home: it shows the people you follow in time order.

Download a copy of your data from Settings → Account security → Download my data. Correct your details in Edit profile.

How long we keep things: read notifications 90 days (any notification 180 days); feed history 30 days; everything tied to your account until you delete it. When you delete your account, usage events are kept only without anything linking them to you, so daily totals stay correct.

Versy is not for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, tell us and we will delete it.
