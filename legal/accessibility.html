Our aim

Versy is built so everyone can vote, enter and follow contests. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA in the app, and we test new screens at larger text sizes before they ship.

Text size

Every word in Versy follows the text size you choose on your phone (iPhone: Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size; Android: Settings → Display → Font size; the names vary by phone), up to twice the standard size. At larger sizes screens rearrange themselves so nothing is cut off: words that sit on pictures move under them, and lists take more lines.

Screen readers

Buttons, pictures and switches are named for VoiceOver and TalkBack, and screen titles are marked as headings. You can vote without dragging: on a matchup, the VS screen offers actions to vote for the top or bottom entry, watch either one fullscreen, skip, go back or undo. Each tile of a contest bracket says who it is, their share of the vote and whether they went through; at larger text sizes the bracket is also listed in words.

Captions

Videos uploaded to Versy get captions made automatically from what is said in them. Turn them on in Settings → Captions. Automatic captions can have mistakes, and videos with music and no speech have none.

Motion and sound

If Reduce Motion is on, Versy shortens or removes its animations and the Versy of the Week cards stop moving on their own. UI sounds can be turned off in Settings → Sounds.

What we are still working on

One label, Delete account in Settings (in our pink), meets the colour contrast Apple and Google set for bold text, but not the higher level WCAG sets for smaller text. Photos and videos are made by members, so we cannot guarantee that what they show or say is accessible.
