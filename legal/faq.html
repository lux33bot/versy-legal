HOW VERSY WORKS

Product copy

How do matchups work?

Every entry in a challenge is paired against others through the day. Voters see two entries, one above the other, and drag the bar onto the one they prefer. You never see the same entry twice in one day’s open round.

Is it fair if I enter late?

Yes. A new entry is served first to voters who have not seen it until it has caught up, and entries are ranked by a confidence model, not by raw vote count, so an entry that arrived at 5 PM with fewer matchups is compared on the same footing as one from 9 AM. Entries close before the cut — two hours by default, longer on a quiet day — so every entry gets enough matchups.

What if I enter after entries close?

Your entry goes into tomorrow’s contest. It sits on your profile marked TOMORROW, visible only to you, and goes public the moment tonight’s winner is crowned — voting on it starts then. Nobody has to wait for midnight.

How can I see how my entry is doing?

Open your entry: the details sheet shows how many matchups it has had and how often it was picked. The category page shows where today is — entering, voting, playoffs or crowned — and when each step comes.

When are the playoffs?

At the cut (8 PM local at launch) the top entries by confidence go into a bracket. Rounds run on a fixed clock so the crown always lands at 10 PM local — never in the middle of the night.

How big is the bracket?

It depends on how many people are voting: brackets grow only when each match can get enough votes to be decided fairly. Small categories run 2- or 4-entry brackets; big ones up to 32.

What happens each month?

On the 1st of each month, the previous month’s daily winners in a category meet in a monthly final that runs on the same engine, and the winner takes the category for that month.

What if an entry does not fit the challenge?

Long-press that one video while voting (or open it and tap •••) and choose “Doesn’t fit the challenge”. When three different people flag the same entry it is held until a moderator checks it.

What is This or That?

A personal post with two photos (“Which outfit?”): pick two photos in My VS, ask your question, choose who can vote (everyone or your followers) and for how long (3 hours, 24 hours or 3 days). Voters tap the one they like and see the split right away. You see the percentages and the number of votes, never who picked what, and you hear the result when it closes. It has no effect on contests or points.

Can I win money?

Entering is always free. Recognition, badges and points are the prizes today. If a contest ever offers a prize, its page will say so and list the rules and eligibility.

How is content moderated?

Every entry and post goes into a review queue, and anyone can report one. When three different people report the same thing it is held until a moderator reviews it. Under-18 accounts are private by default.

How do I report or block someone?

On any entry, open the details sheet and tap ••• for Report or Block. Long-press a comment to report it. On a profile, tap •••. A report takes one tap and a reason; when three different people report the same thing it is hidden until a person reviews it. Blocking someone hides you from each other everywhere — matchups, feeds and comments — and ends any follow between you.
