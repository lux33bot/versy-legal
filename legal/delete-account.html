DELETE YOUR VERSY ACCOUNT

Versy, by Monkeyhead LLC

In the app

Open Versy, go to Settings, tap DELETE ACCOUNT and confirm. It takes effect immediately.

Without the app

To be completed by counsel: The address for deletion requests is being set up; until then, delete from the app as above.

What is deleted

Your account and sign-in, your profile (handle, name, bio, photo, birth year), your entries and posts with their photos and videos, your comments, follows, blocks, notifications and the devices registered for notifications. Photos and videos are removed from our storage and video provider within about an hour.

What is kept

Votes you cast stay so past contest results remain correct, and reports you made stay so moderation decisions hold, both with nothing that links them to you. Usage records (what was shown, watch time) stay only as anonymous counts, with nothing that links them to you. Backups held by our database provider expire on their normal schedule.
