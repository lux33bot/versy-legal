Safety and moderation

Versy has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. Text is filtered automatically when it is posted, every entry goes into a review queue, and we review reports within 24 hours: content that breaks these rules is removed and the account that posted it can be removed too.

Every entry and post goes into a queue our moderators review, and anything reported is looked at by a person. We remove content that breaks these rules and report illegal material where the law requires.

Every photo and video is also checked automatically against known child sexual abuse material before anyone else can see it. A match is blocked, the account is suspended, and the material is kept and reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children as US law requires.

Not allowed: anything illegal; sexual content or nudity; any sexual or exploitative content involving minors (reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children as US law requires); harassment, bullying or hate; threats or glorified violence; dangerous stunts or self-harm; spam, scams or impersonation; someone else’s private information; content you do not have the rights to.

We share information with law enforcement when the law requires it or when someone’s safety is at immediate risk.

Messages

Messages follow the same rules as everything else on Versy. Someone you don’t follow back can send you one text-only request; nothing more reaches you unless you accept or reply, and you can delete, block or report it without them being told. Accounts under 18 can message only people they follow both ways.

Messages are private between the two of you. If you or the other person reports a message, our moderators can read that message (and see a photo in it) to decide on the report. Text in requests and in chats with under-18 accounts is checked automatically for words that break these rules.
